How these listings were collected

Each entry links to a maker's or business's own website. Public names, craft descriptions and locations are summarized from those sources. A market or curated shop is identified as a destination; it is not presented as every maker's workshop.

Before you visit

Luvai Africa is not claiming a partnership or endorsement. Please confirm opening hours, stock and visit arrangements directly with each business. Where an exact maker-workshop address is not public, the listing says so instead of guessing.
